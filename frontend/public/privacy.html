Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Otis Trade ("we", "the site") is an independent trading dashboard for Deriv accounts. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Deriv. This policy explains what information the site handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • The site has no server and no database. It runs entirely in your web browser.
  • Your trading data is stored only in your own browser and is sent only to Deriv.
  • We never see your Deriv password. You sign in on Deriv's own website.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and we never sell data.

1. Information the site handles

None of this information is sent to us. We have no copy of it and cannot access it.

2. Who receives data

  • Deriv (oauth.deriv.com, ws.derivws.com): your browser connects directly to Deriv's official API to log in, read market data and your account, and place trades. Deriv processes this data under Deriv's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site's files. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when loading them. See Google's privacy policy.

3. Automated trading

If you start a bot, it analyses market data in your browser and may place trades on your Deriv account automatically, within the risk limits you set. Real-money trading requires you to type an explicit confirmation for that specific account. Bots run only while the site is open in your browser.

4. How long data is kept

Data stays in your browser until you remove it. Older history is trimmed automatically to stay within browser storage limits.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Sign out removes your Deriv tokens from the browser. Settings → Disconnect Deriv & sign out also asks Deriv to invalidate them.
  • To delete everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings (Site settings → Clear data).
  • You can revoke the site's access at any time from your Deriv account under Security → Connected apps / API token.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 or the EU GDPR) you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because we hold none of your data, these rights are exercised directly in your browser and with Deriv.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, uses a strict Content Security Policy that only allows connections to Deriv, and never transmits your tokens anywhere except Deriv. Anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser profile could use a signed-in session, so sign out on shared computers.

7. Children

The site is not intended for anyone under 18 or under the minimum age required by Deriv to hold an account.

8. Changes

We will update this page if how the site handles data changes, and change the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the site operator privately via GitHub.

Risk warning: trading derivatives and synthetic indices carries a high risk of losing money. Automated strategies can and do lose. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and start on a demo account.
