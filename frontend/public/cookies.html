Cookie Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Otis Trade does not set cookies. It uses your browser's local storage, which works like a cookie but is never sent to any server. Everything it stores is strictly necessary for the site to work, so no consent is required, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies of any kind.

What is stored in your browser

NamePurposeKept until
ot:sessionYour signed-in Deriv accounts and their access tokensYou sign out
dat_sessionMarks this browser as signed inYou sign out
ot:<account>:*Your bot settings, risk limits, signals, trades, orders and activity logYou clear site data (older history is trimmed automatically)
ot:returning, ot:signed_outRemember whether to take you straight to Deriv's login on your next visitYou clear site data
ot:cookie_noticeRemembers that you have seen the storage noticeYou clear site data

Third parties

  • Deriv sets its own cookies on deriv.com when you log in on Deriv's page. Those are governed by Deriv's policies, not this one.
  • Google Fonts delivers the site's typefaces. It does not set cookies for this site but receives your IP address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and does not set tracking cookies.

Removing stored data

Sign out to remove your Deriv tokens. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser (for example in Chrome: padlock icon → Site settings → Clear data).
